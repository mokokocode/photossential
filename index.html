Quick edits

Analyzes the photo and nudges its colors so the overall average reads as neutral gray -- corrects a cast from indoor lighting, shade, or an overcast sky. Applied before film presets and the sliders below, so it works well combined with either. Like any "auto" white balance, it can occasionally be fooled by a photo that's dominated by one real color (a sunset, a green field filling the frame) -- turn it off if a photo looks worse with it.
